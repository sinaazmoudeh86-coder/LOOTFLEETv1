The Brand

Loot Fleet is a free idle space-ARPG where the loot never stops: blast alien swarms, evolve a lone frigate into a titan fleet, and conquer a living galaxy — even while you're away.

Naming
LOOT FLEET

The game. Two words, always caps in display contexts. In body copy: "Loot Fleet". Never "Lootfleet" or "LootFleet" for the game itself.

LootCoins

The premium currency. One word, camel-case, always plural. Always paired with the coin mark when space allows.

LootFleet Pro

The subscription. One word here — it reads as a product line. Benefits language: "⚡ 5× speed · ✨ 2× XP".

Descriptors

"Idle Space ARPG" (primary) · "the loot never stops" (flavor) · "free · browser · no download" (proof points).

Taglines

Primary: Farm the galaxy. Build the fleet.
Support: The loot never stops. · Raze citadels. Claim the stars. · Even while you're away.

Wordmark

The wordmark is set in Orbitron Black, two-tone: LOOT in Cargo Gold, FLEET in Ion Cyan — each with its soft signature glow. It lives on deep-space darks, always.

LOOTFLEET
Do
  • Use on Void/Deep-Navy backgrounds (or photography darker than #20262f)
  • Keep the two-tone split exactly at LOOT|FLEET
  • Keep clear space of one "O" height on all sides
  • Use the glow at low opacity (≤50%) — it's an aura, not a lens flare
Don't
  • Place on white or light backgrounds
  • Recolor, outline, or single-tone the mark
  • Stretch, italicize, or change the letterspacing
  • Set body copy in Orbitron — it's display-only

Typography

Two faces, strict roles. Orbitron is the voice of the machine — headlines, scores, labels. Rajdhani does the talking — UI, body, numbers.

Display · Orbitron 700–900 · caps · tracking +4–10%
FLEET SCORE 247B
⚡ HOLD THE GALAXY
UI & body · Rajdhani 500–700
Deploy to Zone 7, raze the Void Citadel, and tow the spoils home. Every kill drops loot — the magnet does the rest.
Numbers always tabular: 29.16K · +14/h · 3.63Qa
Rules
  • Big number energy: scores and damage are the largest thing on screen
  • Unit ladder for big numbers: K · M · B · T · Qa · Qi (never scientific notation)
  • Labels in caps with wide tracking; sentence case for everything conversational

Iconography & Motifs

Hexagons everywhere — the galaxy is a hex grid, the LootCoin is a hex coin, shield cores are hexes. Glow means value: the rarer, the brighter.

The LootCoin mark

Hex coin, gold-to-violet, with a loot-gem facet cut into the center — the currency literally contains the loot players chase. Never recolor, never flatten the gradient, never place on light backgrounds. Minimum size 14px; pair with amounts in Rajdhani 700.

Supporting glyph set

⬢ Fuel · ◆ Iron · ✦ Plasma · ⚡ speed/power · ⚔ combat · ⛴ citadel · ☠ boss · ★ featured/owned · ⌂ home — keep glyphs in their economy colors.

Ship & fortress art

The starter Frigate — "day one" energy

The Mothership — endgame aspiration

The Void Citadel — the enemy monument

  • Ships float on glows/shadows, never sit flat; nose-up or 3/4 hero angles
  • Progression pairs sell the fantasy: frigate → titan, before → after

Voice & Tone

You're the fleet's operations officer: confident, punchy, a little gallows-humor about the swarm. Imperatives over descriptions. Numbers over adjectives.

"Raze the citadel. Keep the loot."
✓ Imperative, concrete, loot-first
"Experience an epic space adventure!"
✕ Generic mobile-ad slop
"224 kills. 357 plasma. Zero effort — you were asleep."
✓ Numbers + idle fantasy + wink
"Don't miss this amazing limited offer!!"
✕ Pressure language; we never beg
"⚠ SUPER BOSS — premium loot · clear it for the big drops"
✓ Threat + reward in one breath
"Purchase virtual currency to enhance gameplay"
✕ Legal-brochure tone. Say "LootCoins buy cosmetics — never power."
House rules
  • Player is "operator" or "pilot" — never "user"
  • Monetization is honest and proud: "cosmetics only — never power"
  • Emoji-glyphs (⚡⛴☠★) are punctuation, max 1–2 per line

Social & Campaign Use

Every asset should look like a frame of the game: deep-space dark, one gold hero element, big numbers, loot glow.

The formula
Background

Void → Deep Navy radial; starfield optional. Never white, never daylight.

Hero element

One ship / coin / citadel with its glow. One per asset — let it breathe.

Headline

Orbitron caps, ≤6 words, gold or two-tone. Body in Rajdhani.

CTA

"Play free in your browser" → lootfleet.com. Gold pill button.

Ready-made hooks

"Farm the galaxy. Build the fleet." · "The loot never stops." · "From one frigate to a fleet of titans." · "Your fleet fought while you slept." · "12 rarity tiers. One obsession."

Checklist before posting
  • Wordmark two-tone and legible at thumbnail size
  • Rarity colors used in correct order (no purple "Legendary")
  • LootCoin mark unaltered; monetization copy includes "never power"
  • Screenshot assets use real gameplay — the game looks this good, show it
