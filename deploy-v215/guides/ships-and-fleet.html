Every Ship in Loot Fleet — Hull Tier List & Fleet Building

Thirteen hulls stand between your starter Frigate and the Mothership. Here's what each one brings, how unlocks actually work, and how the level-100 fleet system turns retired ships into wingmates.

Updated June 2026 · 8 min read

How hull unlocks work

Every hull (after your free Frigate) needs three things: its blueprint (dropped by the boss of a specific zone — shown on the ship card), a kill count proven in the previous hull, and the gold price. No shortcuts — except the two hero-banner offers below.

Fast-track: the Ships tab carries a rotating hero offer — the Carrier for 25,000 LootCoins, then (once you own a Carrier) the Mothership for 75,000. Skips blueprint and kill requirements entirely. Cosmetics never sell power, but hulls you could earn anyway are fair game for impatience.

The fleet system: your old ships fly again

From level 100, escort slots unlock every 100 levels (Lv 100/200/300/400) — flagship plus four escorts, five ships max, no duplicates. Escorts fly in formation, fire real class-matched shots at 25% of your damage, and contribute 30% of their hull bonuses to your Fleet Score.

  • An escort Aegis doesn't shoot — it emits repair pulses that heal your flagship mid-fight.
  • Fleet picks show on the leaderboard — tap any pilot to inspect their fleet and flagship loadout.
  • Manage it all in Hangar → My Fleet: tap an open slot, pick any owned hull, done.

Which hull should you fly?

  • Leveling: ride the gold ladder — each hull is simply better than the last.
  • Wave Zones / deep space: Carrier line. Drones are free DPS against swarms.
  • Survival builds: Aegis + a high-rarity Warden Array = doubled fleet aura (heal, damage reduction, +range).
  • Endgame: Mothership — its 7-hardpoint mixed volley with a 45% range bonus is the game's power ceiling.
