How to Play Loot Fleet — The Complete Beginner's Guide

Loot Fleet is an idle space ARPG: your ship fights automatically, loot drops constantly, and your job is the fun part — deciding what to equip, where to push, and what to conquer. Here's your first hour, explained.

Updated June 2026 · 8 min read

The 30-second version

  1. Deploy to Zone 1. Your ship auto-targets and auto-fires. In the opening zones you'll nearly one-shot everything, and every kill drops loot.
  2. Loot flies to you. A magnet pulls drops to your hull. Tap any item in your Loot hold to compare and equip — or turn on Auto-Equip and let the ship dress itself.
  3. Push deeper. When a zone feels easy, deploy to the next. Deeper zones = harder enemies = better loot, gated by a rarity ladder that keeps drops honest.
  4. Open the Galaxy. Once you find your feet, the real game begins: 1,950 hex tiles to capture, resources to farm, and other players to out-grab.

Your ship fights — you command

Combat is fully automatic: your ship picks targets, fires every equipped weapon hardpoint in rotation, and drones (on carrier hulls) engage on their own. You steer the strategy layer:

  • Battle — watch the fight, grab the loot, swap speeds (1×–3× free).
  • Zone Grind — pick your hunting ground. Higher zones scale enemy level and loot quality.
  • Loot — your cargo hold. 100 slots to start; full hold auto-scraps new drops into resources, so sell junk often.
  • Hangar — your ship, its equipment slots, the ship store, market and cosmetics.
  • My Galaxy — the conquest map. More on this below.

Loot: the entire point

Every enemy can drop gear for six slot types — Cannon, Munitions, Hull, Thrusters, Targeting and Shield Core. Items roll a rarity tier from Common up to Primordial, and rarity is progression-gated: Zone 1–4 caps at Rare, Divine doesn't exist until Zone 55+. If something glows, walk over it.

Tip: Weapons belong to one of six classes — laser, gatling, missile, rail, plasma, Warden — each with a built-in signature bonus. Mixed arsenals fire mixed volleys, and it looks as good as it sounds.

Leveling and the speed of the early game

Zones 1–5 are the fast lane: paper-thin enemies, 100% drop rates, instant gratification. From Zone 6 the real grind begins, and from there the curve is deliberately long — levels are earned, with milestone walls at 100/200/300 that gate fleet slots.

Ships: frigate today, mothership someday

You start in a Frigate. Bigger hulls add weapon hardpoints (up to 7), armor slots, drone bays and built-in stat bonuses. Most hulls unlock the honest way — recover their blueprint from a zone boss, prove yourself in the previous hull, then pay gold. Read the full hull tier list.

The Galaxy: where the endgame lives

The My Galaxy map is a massive hex grid radiating from a neutral Home Citadel. Capturing tiles earns hourly resources (⬢ fuel, ◆ iron, ✦ plasma) that pay for deeper warps. Boss tiles, 100×-output Citadel tiles, deep-space risk/reward, and a live turf war against other players — it's a 4X game hiding inside an idle ARPG.

Frequently asked questions

Is Loot Fleet free?

Yes — free in your browser, no download. LootCoins (the premium currency) buy cosmetics and convenience like a 4× speed unlock. Never combat power.

Does it progress while I'm offline?

Yes. Kills, loot and territory income continue while you're away, with a welcome-back summary.

Do I need an account?

No. Guest play is instant. A free account adds cloud saves across devices and entry into the real cross-player turf war and leaderboard.

What's the best first goal?

Unlock the Interceptor (your first new hull), then capture your first ring-1 galaxy tile. After that, the game will be making goals for you.
