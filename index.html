About the game

What is Loot Fleet?

Loot Fleet is a free idle space game — an ARPG you play right in your browser with no download and no install. Your fleet auto-battles through endless bullet-hell zones while procedural loot pours in across fourteen rarity tiers, from Common all the way to Artifact. Every upgrade pushes you a zone deeper; every zone drops something rarer.

It's also a space strategy and mining game: capture territory in a living 1,950-tile galaxy, terraform five moon colonies that mine resources 24/7, and defend your Home Citadel in tower-defense waves where you build lasers, cryo spires and rail cannons during the fight. Manufacture new hulls in the Shipworks, hunt the weekly Dreadnaught to grow your permanent Pilot Tree, and take on server-wide season raid bosses with every other player.

Progress never stops — Loot Fleet keeps farming while you're offline, and one free account syncs your save across phone, tablet and desktop. New to idle ARPGs? Start with the beginner's guide or just press play — you'll be looting in ten seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Loot Fleet free to play?

Yes — Loot Fleet is completely free to play in your browser. You can even start as a guest with no signup. Optional LootCoin packs exist for players who want to speed things up, but everything in the game can be earned by playing.

Do I need to download anything to play?

No download, no install. Loot Fleet runs in any modern browser on desktop, laptop, tablet or phone. You can also add it to your home screen as an app and keep playing offline.

What kind of game is Loot Fleet?

Loot Fleet is an idle space ARPG — part incremental game, part action RPG, part strategy. Your fleet auto-battles and farms loot on its own, while you make the big calls: gear, ships, skills, moon colonies, citadel defense and galaxy conquest.

Does the game progress while I'm offline?

Yes. Your operator keeps farming kills, XP and loot while you're away, your moon colonies mine around the clock, and your Home Citadel stockpiles ore. Come back, collect the haul, and push deeper.

Can I play on multiple devices?

Yes — create one free account and your save syncs to the cloud. Start a session on your PC, continue on your iPad or phone; your fleet, loot and progress follow you everywhere.

How many ships are in Loot Fleet?

Thirty-one hulls, from the starter Frigate to the final-class Titan Sina — unlocked through boss blueprints, galaxy resources, Shipworks part crates, mission rewards, the alliance Monolith Shipyard and season-event exclusives.
